Fry: Efficient Chess Transformer →

A chess transformer with a new architecture (SwiGLU, QK-Norm, chess-specific positional encoding, learned board-relation features). Playing from a single forward pass, Fry is within 51 Elo of Leela Chess Zero's BT4 on about a quarter of its training compute. A smaller 23M-parameter version plays BT4 close to even with search. The site also covers interpretability experiments on its attention heads.
